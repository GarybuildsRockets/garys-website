How to Play

  1. Select Single Player mode or create/join a Multiplayer Lobby.
  2. Open the provided technical drawing schematic on your browser.
  3. Recreate the part in SolidWorks, Fusion 360, or Onshape.
  4. Assign the standard specified material (e.g., 6061-T6 Aluminum).
  5. Open Mass Properties in your CAD software and calculate the mass.
  6. Submit your answer before the stopwatch or opponent beats you! (3 attempts max)
