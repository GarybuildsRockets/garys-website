Engineering Context & Workflow

In Formula SAE / Motorsport suspension tuning, Lotus Shark (LSA) provides detailed kinematic sweeps for hardpoint positioning. This automated tool bypasses the native low-resolution screenshot constraints by taking copied tabular results, auto-detecting delimiters (tabs or commas), and outputting crisp vector graphics optimized for FSAE design reports.
