F1 Aerodynamics Physics & Mathematical Model

1. Total Aerodynamic Downforce ($F_L$) & Drag ($F_D$)

F_L = 0.5 · ρ · v² · A_ref · C_L(α, h)
F_D = 0.5 · ρ · v² · A_ref · C_D(α, h)

Where:
• ρ (Fluid Density): Standard sea-level air density = $1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3$.
• v (Velocity): Freestream air velocity relative to car ($\text{m/s}$).
• A_ref (Frontal Area): Standard F1 reference frontal area = $1.60 \text{ m}^2$.
• C_L / C_D: Non-dimensional Lift and Drag Coefficients synthesized from front wing, rear wing, and floor ground effect.

2. Underfloor Ground-Effect Venturi Channel Suction

p_1 + 0.5 · ρ · v_1² = p_2 + 0.5 · ρ · v_2²   (Bernoulli's Principle)
C_{L,floor} = C_{L0,floor} · (h_0 / h_{avg})^{0.65} · \cos(\theta_{pitch})

Ground effect downforce relies on Bernoulli's principle and Venturi throat constriction. As underbody clearance ($h_{avg}$) decreases toward optimum seal point ($~25\text{mm}$), underbody velocity ($v_2$) accelerates relative to freestream ($v_1$), dropping static pressure ($p_2$) underneath the floor and sucking the chassis down to the asphalt.

3. Wing-Induced Drag ($C_{D,induced}$)

C_{D,induced} = (C_{L,wing}²) / (π · AR · e)

Where AR is the wing Aspect Ratio ($b^2 / S$) and e is the Oswald planform efficiency factor ($~0.82$). High wing angle of attack increases $C_L$, generating proportional wingtip vortex shedding and induced drag penalty.

4. Aerodynamic Balance & Power Overcoming Drag

Balance_{\% front} = (F_{L,front} / F_{L,total}) · 100\%
Power_{req} = F_D · v   (\text{Watts})

Aero balance determines high-speed cornering stability. Power required to overcome aerodynamic drag scales with the cube of velocity ($v^3$), placing immense demand on modern hybrid power units at speeds exceeding $300\text{ km/h}$.
