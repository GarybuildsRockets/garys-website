F1 Aerodynamics Physics & Mathematical Model

1. Aerodynamic Downforce ($F_L$) & Drag ($F_D$)

F_L = 0.5 · ρ · v² · A_ref · C_L(α, h)
F_D = 0.5 · ρ · v² · A_ref · C_D(α, h)

Where:
• ρ (Air Density): Standard density = $1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3$.
• v (Air Velocity): Freestream wind speed.
• A_ref (Reference Area): F1 reference frontal area = $1.60 \text{ m}^2$.

2. Ground Effect Venturi Suction

p_1 + 0.5 · ρ · v_1² = p_2 + 0.5 · ρ · v_2²   (Bernoulli's Principle)
C_{L,floor} = C_{L0,floor} · (h_0 / h_{avg})^{0.65}

As underbody ride clearance drops, Venturi channel constriction accelerates underbody airflow, decreasing static pressure under the floor and sucking the chassis down to the ground.
