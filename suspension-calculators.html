Suspension & Chassis Calculators

Interactive engineering calculators backed by foundational methodologies from *Race Car Design* (Derek Seward) and *Race Car Vehicle Dynamics* (Milliken & Milliken).

1. Centre of Mass & Static Loads

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Static Rear Load: W_R = W × (l_m / L)[cite: 5, 8]
Static Front Load: W_F = W - W_R[cite: 8]
Ref: Derek Seward, "Race Car Design", Chapter 4 (Mass and Dimension Properties)[cite: 5, 6, 8].

2. Longitudinal Load Transfer

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Load Transfer: ΔW_x = (W × a_x × h_m) / (L × g)[cite: 11, 16]
Ref: Milliken & Milliken, "Race Car Vehicle Dynamics", Chapter 16 (Longitudinal Weight Transfer)[cite: 11, 13, 16, 17].

3. Lateral Load Transfer (Cornering)

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Total Lateral Transfer: ΔW_y = (W × a_y × h_m) / T[cite: 20]
Ref: Milliken & Milliken, "Race Car Vehicle Dynamics", Chapter 15 (Lateral Weight Transfer)[cite: 20].

4. Anti-Dive / Anti-Squat Calculator

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Percent Anti: % Anti = (H_IC × L / h_m) × 100%[cite: 73]
Ref: Derek Seward, "Race Car Design", Chapter 8 (Suspension Geometry & Anti-Dive/Squat)[cite: 71, 72, 73].

5. Suspension Pushrod Force Analyzer

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Pushrod Force: F_p = W_vert / sin(θ)[cite: 80]
Ref: Derek Seward, "Race Car Design", Chapter 7 (Structural Analysis of Chassis and Suspension Components)[cite: 47, 80, 81, 84].

6. Impact Attenuator & Crush Calculator

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Kinetic Energy: E_k = 0.5 × m × u²[cite: 57]
Min Length: L_min = (u² / (2 × g_max × 9.81)) / 0.7[cite: 58]
Ref: Derek Seward, "Race Car Design", Chapter 3 (Safety and Regulations) / Formula SAE Impact Attenuator Guidelines[cite: 57, 58, 59].

7. Wheel Upright Force & Vector Analyzer

🧮 View Formula & Reference
Braking Force: F_x = W_dyn × a_x
Cornering Force: F_y = W_dyn × a_y
Resultant Vector: F_res = √(F_x² + F_y²)
Self-Aligning Torque: M_z = F_y × \text{Trail}
Tie-Rod Load: F_tierod = M_z / L_arm
Ref: Milliken & Milliken, "Race Car Vehicle Dynamics", Chapter on Steering & Tire Aligning Torque.
